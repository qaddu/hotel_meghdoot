01 • Hotel History

A Legacy Built on Warmth & Trust

Located near Post Office Line in Gangolihat, Hotel Meghdoot & Restaurant was envisioned as a serene home for pilgrims, families, and Himalayan explorers seeking authentic Kumaoni hospitality.

“In the hills of Kumaon, a guest is never a stranger—they arrive as a traveler and leave as part of our family.”

— Hotel Meghdoot Heritage Philosophy
Chapter I • The Beginning

A Sanctuary for Sacred Pilgrims

Established to welcome devotees traveling from across India to Maa Haat Kalika Shaktipeeth and the mystical caves of Patal Bhuvaneshwar, offering spotless rooms and 24/7 hot water in Gangolihat.

Chapter II • Culinary Identity

Birth of Pahadi Rasoi & Bhutwa Poori

Our kitchen evolved into a beloved Gangolihat culinary landmark—celebrated for our signature slow-cooked Bhutwa Poori, pure vegetarian Kumaoni Thalis, and a dedicated Separate Kitchen for Jain & yatra groups.

Chapter III • Today & Beyond

Celebrations & Himalayan Expeditions

Today, Hotel Meghdoot is a complete destination featuring a grand Banquet & Conference Hall for Kumaoni weddings and an experienced travel desk organizing Adi Kailash, Darma Valley, Munsiyari, and Chaukori tours.
