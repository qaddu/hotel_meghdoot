Maa Haat Kalika

Shrine 01 • Heart of Gangolihat

Maa Haat Kalika Mandir

Just a peaceful 5-minute drive or scenic forest walk from Hotel Meghdoot stands the revered Maa Haat Kalika Shaktipeeth. Surrounded by towering ancient deodar trees, this sacred shrine was consecrated by Adi Shankaracharya and is deeply venerated as the patron deity (Ishta Devi) of the Kumaon Regiment of the Indian Army.

Devotees experience a profound stillness beneath the canopy of cedar trees, where copper temple bells, morning incense, and evening Aarti create an atmosphere of pure devotion.

Distance ~1.5 km 5 mins from Hotel
Best Darshan 6:00 – 8:30 AM & Evening Aarti
Major Festivals Navratri Chaitra & Sharad
Shrine 02 • Mystical Cave of Lord Shiva

Patal Bhuvaneshwar Mandir

Descend 90 feet beneath the earth into a 160-meter-long natural limestone cave that feels suspended in mythic time. Chronicled in the Manaskhand of the Skanda Purana, Patal Bhuvaneshwar is revered as the sacred underground abode where 33 crore deities reside alongside Lord Shiva.

Guided by local temple priests, pilgrims witness extraordinary natural rock formations of Sheshnag holding the earth, the Four Yuga Pillars (Satyug, Tretayug, Dwaparyug, and Kaliyug), Adi Ganesha, and the mystical gateway to Mount Kailash.

Distance ~14 km 35 mins scenic drive
Cave Descent ~90 Feet Guided priest entry
Pilgrim Tip Morning Visit Wear comfortable socks

Maa Kotgadi Mandir

Shrine 03 • Goddess of Justice

Maa Kotgadi Mandir

Perched gracefully amidst Kumaon's emerald hills, Maa Kotgadi (Kotgadi Devi) is revered across Uttarakhand as Nyaya Ki Devi—the Goddess of Justice and Truth. Pilgrims from distant valleys travel here with heartfelt prayers, trusting in her swift grace and protection.

The scenic drive from Gangolihat winds through pine forests and terraced Kumaoni villages, making it a deeply uplifting half-day spiritual excursion paired effortlessly with Patal Bhuvaneshwar.

Distance ~22 km 50 mins from Hotel
Atmosphere Serene Hills Panoramic vistas
Best Combined 1-Day Circuit All 3 Shrines
