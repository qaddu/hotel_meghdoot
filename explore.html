Garhwal Himalayas • Jyotirlinga

Kedarnath Yatra

Embark on a deeply spiritual pilgrimage to Shri Kedarnath Dham (3,583 m) dedicated to Lord Shiva. Our travel desk arranges comfortable hill vehicles, scenic halts through Rudraprayag & Guptkashi, hotel stays, and complete trek or helicopter guidance from Gaurikund.

Elevation 3,583 Meters
Trek Distance 16 km (Gaurikund)
Ideal For Pilgrims & Families

Key Highlights

  • Shri Kedarnath Temple & Evening Aarti
  • Guptkashi Vishwanath & Sonprayag
  • Yatra Registration & Mule/Palki Guidance
  • Pure Veg / Sattvik Meal Planning

Day 1: Depart Gangolihat / Kathgodam → Drive via Karnaprayag to Guptkashi / Sitapur (Overnight Stay).

Day 2: Early shuttle to Gaurikund → 16 km trek / helicopter to Kedarnath Dham → Evening Darshan & Aarti.

Day 3: Morning Abhishek Darshan → Descend to Sonprayag → Rest at Guptkashi.

Day 4: Return scenic drive via Rudraprayag & Kausani back to Gangolihat / Drop point.

Customizable cabs, stays & meals
Char Dham Circuit • Alaknanda Valley

Badrinath Yatra

Travel along the sacred Alaknanda River to Shri Badrinath Dham, one of India's four supreme Char Dham shrines. Enjoy road-accessible darshan for elders and families, paired with Mana (India's first village), Tapt Kund, and Joshimath.

Elevation 3,133 Meters
Accessibility Direct Car Access
Combo Option Do Dham Available

Key Highlights

  • Shri Badrinath Temple & Tapt Kund
  • Mana Village, Vyas Gufa & Bhim Pul
  • Joshimath Narsingh Temple & Auli View
  • Senior-Citizen Friendly Comfort Cabs

Day 1: Drive from Gangolihat via Bageshwar & Karnaprayag to Joshimath / Pipalkoti.

Day 2: Morning drive to Badrinath Dham → Holy dip in Tapt Kund, Temple Darshan & Mana Village excursion.

Day 3: Morning Shringar Darshan → Scenic return drive via Auli / Nanda Prayag.

Day 4: Return to Gangolihat or onward drop to Haldwani / Rishikesh.

Combine with Kedarnath for Do Dham

Chopta Valley • Panch Kedar

Tungnath & Chandrashila

Ascend through the lush rhododendron forests of Chopta ("Mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand") to reach Tungnath—the highest Shiva temple in the world—and continue 1.5 km to Chandrashila Summit for an unforgettable 360° Himalayan sunrise.

Summit Height 4,000 Meters
Trek Length 5 km (Moderate)
Bonus Stop Deoria Tal Lake

Key Highlights

  • World's Highest Shiva Temple (Tungnath)
  • Chandrashila 360° Sunrise Panorama
  • Chopta Alpine Meadows & Camping/Stays
  • Optional Deoria Tal & Ukhimath Omkareshwar

Day 1: Scenic drive from Gangolihat via Gwaldam & Gopeshwar to Chopta Meadows (Camp / Lodge stay).

Day 2: Early morning 3.5 km trek to Tungnath Temple + 1.5 km summit push to Chandrashila → Return to Chopta.

Day 3: Optional Deoria Tal visit → Scenic drive back.

Great for couples, friends & trekkers
Most Requested from Gangolihat • Pithoragarh

Adi Kailash & Om Parvat Yatra

Hotel Meghdoot in Gangolihat is the ideal sacred starting base for your Adi Kailash (Chota Kailash) & Om Parvat expedition. After seeking blessings at Maa Haat Kalika & Patal Bhuvaneshwar, journey with our 4x4 fleet and full Inner Line Permit assistance through Dharchula, Gunji, Jolingkong, and Nabhidhang.

Permits ILP Handled by Us
Vehicle 4x4 Bolero / Camper
Best Window May–Jun & Sep–Nov

Key Highlights

  • Adi Kailash Darshan & Parvati Sarovar
  • Miraculous Om Parvat View (Nabhidhang)
  • Kali Mata Temple, Kalapani & Vyas Valley
  • Homestays, Medical Guidance & Meals

Day 1: Stay at Hotel Meghdoot, Gangolihat → Darshan at Maa Haat Kalika & Patal Bhuvaneshwar.

Day 2: Drive to Dharchula → Inner Line Permit verification & evening at Kali River.

Day 3: Scenic 4x4 drive through Chhiyalekh & Garbyang to Gunji / Nabi Village.

Day 4: Jolingkong → Adi Kailash Darshan, Shiva-Parvati Temple & Parvati Sarovar.

Day 5: Nabhidhang for Om Parvat Darshan & Kalapani → Return to Dharchula.

Day 6: Return via Pithoragarh / Gangolihat with cherished memories.

Complete Gangolihat-to-Jolingkong Support

05 • Hidden Valley
Duration: 4–5 Days Apr–Jun & Sep–Nov
Panchachuli Views • Untouched Kumaon

Darma Valley

Discover one of Uttarakhand's most pristine alpine valleys. Drive along the gushing Dhauliganga River to the heritage villages of Dugtu, Dantu, and Sela, and hike effortlessly to the majestic Panchachuli Glacier Base Camp.

Key Villages Dugtu, Dantu & Sela
Glacier Hike 3 km Easy Trail
Experience Bhotiya Culture

Key Highlights

  • Close-up Views of Five Panchachuli Peaks
  • Panchachuli Base Camp Meadows
  • Authentic Wooden Homestays in Dugtu
  • Stargazing & Alpine River Trails

Day 1: Gangolihat → Dharchula (Overnight stay & permits).

Day 2: Drive along Dhauliganga via Sobla & Sela to Dugtu / Dantu Village.

Day 3: Guided trek to Panchachuli Glacier Base Camp → Sunset photography & bonfire evening.

Day 4: Return drive to Dharchula / Gangolihat.

Can be combined with Adi Kailash
Little Kashmir of Uttarakhand • Kumaon Classic

Munsiyari (Khaliya Top & Birthi Fall)

An enchanting high-altitude hill station framed by the gleaming Panchachuli snow massif. Stop at the dramatic 126-meter Birthi Waterfall en route, visit Nanda Devi Temple, and trek up to the panoramic alpine meadow of Khaliya Top.

From Gangolihat ~120 km Scenic Drive
Khaliya Top 3,500 m Meadow
Ideal For Couples & Snow Lovers

Key Highlights

  • Birthi Waterfall & Kalamuni Top Pass
  • Khaliya Top Zero-Point Alpine Trek
  • Nanda Devi Temple & Tribal Heritage Museum
  • Winter Snowfall & Summer Rhododendrons

Day 1: Depart Hotel Meghdoot Gangolihat → Chaukori → Birthi Waterfall → Kalamuni Top → Munsiyari.

Day 2: Guided day hike to Khaliya Top Meadow → Evening at Nanda Devi Temple for golden sunset.

Day 3: Tribal Museum, Thamri Kund & scenic return drive.

Perfect 3-day extension from Gangolihat

Dharchula

Set along the emerald banks of the Kali River facing its twin town in Nepal, Dharchula is a vibrant cultural hub steeped in Rung & Shauka heritage. Enjoy riverside strolls, Narayan Ashram, Jauljibi confluence, and easy access to high-altitude valleys.

From Gangolihat ~145 km Drive
River Kali & Gori Sangam
Culture Border Bazaars

Key Highlights

  • Kali River Suspension Bridge (India–Nepal)
  • Serene Narayan Ashram (44 km uphill)
  • Jauljibi Sangam & Handwoven Woollens
  • Gateway to Adi Kailash & Darma Valley
Scenic river-valley getaway
Cross-Border Escape • Western Nepal

Nepal Tour

Step across the scenic Indo-Nepal border from Pithoragarh / Jhulaghat or Tanakpur into the rich cultural hills of western Nepal. From day excursions to multi-day Pokhara, Kathmandu, and Pashupatinath circuits, we organize smooth border crossings and comfortable transport.

Entry Gateways Jhulaghat / Banbasa
Documents Indian ID Guidance
Flexible Plans 1 Day to 7 Days

Key Highlights

  • Jhulaghat Suspension Bridge & Baitadi Hills
  • Tripura Sundari & Shuklaphanta Wildlife
  • Extended Pokhara & Kathmandu Circuits
  • End-to-End Border & Vehicle Assistance

The Chaukori Hill Station

Just a refreshing 1.5-hour scenic drive from Hotel Meghdoot, Chaukori is a peaceful Himalayan bowl famed for its lush heritage tea estates, pine-scented air, and glowing golden sunset views over Nanda Devi, Nanda Kot, and Panchachuli.

Distance ~46 km (1.5 hrs)
Elevation 2,010 Meters
Best For Same-Day Excursion

Key Highlights

  • Scenic Tea Garden Walks & Tasting
  • Unobstructed Nanda Devi & Panchachuli Views
  • Berinag, Kapileshwar Mahadev & Nag Mandir
  • Easy Day Trip Returning to Hotel Meghdoot
