Land of Faith

The Divine Abode

Awaken to temple bells and misty deodar forests—Hotel Meghdoot places you moments away from Kumaon’s most revered sacred shrines.

~1.5 km • Shaktipeeth

Maa Haat Kalika Mandir

Revered Shaktipeeth consecrated by Adi Shankaracharya and patron deity of the Kumaon Regiment, nestled amid ancient deodars.

~14 km from Hotel

Patal Bhuvaneshwar Mandir

Mystical underground limestone cave temple of Lord Shiva, revered in the Skanda Purana as the sacred abode of 33 crore deities.

~22 km • Devi Shrine

Maa Kotgadi Mandir

Deeply revered across Kumaon as the Goddess of Justice, offering profound spiritual peace and panoramic mountain vistas.
