No shortcuts, no artificial colors—just heirloom mountain grains, hand-pounded spices, and piping hot plates.

Pahadi Harvest

Black Himalayan Bhatt, organic Madua millet, wild Jakhiya seeds, and crisp Gangolihat vegetables picked fresh every morning.

02. Slow Simmered

Iron Kadhai Roasting

Our signature Bhutwa and Kumaoni dals simmer low and slow in seasoned iron cookware for deep, earthy mountain aroma.

03. Pure & Hygienic

Dedicated Veg & Jain Prep

Strict separation between vegetarian, Jain (no onion/garlic), and non-veg counters so every diner eats with total peace of mind.
